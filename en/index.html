How we work

From the first conversation to fitted furniture

Four clear steps – you always know what is happening with your order.

  1. 01

    Consultation & measuring

    We discuss your needs and ideas and measure the room. The consultation is free.

  2. 02

    Design & 3D visualisation

    We prepare the furniture design and a 3D view, so you can see the result before production starts.

  3. 03

    Manufacturing

    Once you approve the design, we build it in our own workshop from the materials chosen together with you.

  4. 04

    Delivery & installation

    We deliver the furniture and install it in your home – all that is left is to enjoy it.
